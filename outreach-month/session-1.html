Outreach Month Training

Session 1: Personal Evangelism — Theology

This training session reviews, straight from the Scriptures, real examples of personal evangelism and applies them to our day. There are people in the Bible just like the people all around us — and the same God who reached them is ready to reach our generation through us.

Our Text

John 1:35-51 (NKJV)

35 Again, the next day, John stood with two of his disciples.

36 And looking at Jesus as He walked, he said, "Behold the Lamb of God!"

37 The two disciples heard him speak, and they followed Jesus.

38 Then Jesus turned, and seeing them following, said to them, "What do you seek?" They said to Him, "Rabbi" (which is to say, when translated, Teacher), "where are You staying?"

39 He said to them, "Come and see." They came and saw where He was staying, and remained with Him that day (now it was about the tenth hour).

40 One of the two who heard John speak, and followed Him, was Andrew, Simon Peter's brother.

41 He first found his own brother Simon, and said to him, "We have found the Messiah" (which is translated, the Christ).

42 And he brought him to Jesus. Now when Jesus looked at him, He said, "You are Simon the son of Jonah. You shall be called Cephas" (which is translated, A Stone).

43 The following day Jesus wanted to go to Galilee, and He found Philip and said to him, "Follow Me."

44 Now Philip was from Bethsaida, the city of Andrew and Peter.

45 Philip found Nathanael and said to him, "We have found Him of whom Moses in the law, and also the prophets, wrote—Jesus of Nazareth, the son of Joseph."

46 And Nathanael said to him, "Can anything good come out of Nazareth?" Philip said to him, "Come and see."

47 Jesus saw Nathanael coming toward Him, and said of him, "Behold, an Israelite indeed, in whom is no deceit!"

48 Nathanael said to Him, "How do You know me?" Jesus answered and said to him, "Before Philip called you, when you were under the fig tree, I saw you."

49 Nathanael answered and said to Him, "Rabbi, You are the Son of God! You are the King of Israel!"

50 Jesus answered and said to him, "Because I said to you, 'I saw you under the fig tree,' do you believe? You will see greater things than these."

51 And He said to him, "Most assuredly, I say to you, hereafter you shall see heaven open, and the angels of God ascending and descending upon the Son of Man."

Purpose

Learn directly from the Scriptures how to reach individuals with the gospel.

  • If we can do it one-on-one successfully, then other forms of outreach and evangelism become a bonus.
  • There are biblical examples that mirror our real-life experiences today.

Why We Will Succeed

  • Jesus commanded us to go. The Great Commission is His command, not a suggestion. Mark 16:15-18
  • We are designed to believe in something higher than ourselves. It is our default setting — it is why people believe one thing or another. In fact, believing in nothing is itself a belief.
  • There is always someone ready. There is always a ripe fruit waiting on you to lead them to Christ. John 4:35
  • The word of God will grow and win. Acts 19:20
  • Those appointed to salvation will hear and be saved. Acts 13:48

Goal

  • Focus on the personal evangelism and outreach examples.
  • Become proficient through practice.
  • Practicing what we believe will make us congregationally effective in other forms of outreach.
  • Each one of us becomes a personal evangelist.

Scripture Examples

There are people like these all around us, and we will find them by the grace of God — and they will be ready. Our text above is the first example: Andrew and Philip, who found Peter and Nathanael and brought them to Jesus.

Andrew and Philip

John 1:40-51 — Ordinary disciples who immediately went and brought their own brother and friend to Jesus. The model of one-on-one personal evangelism.

Lydia

Acts 16:13-15 — A businesswoman at the place of prayer whose heart the Lord opened to heed the things spoken by Paul.

The Eunuch from Ethiopia

Acts 8:26-40 — A seeker already reading the Scriptures, to whom Philip was sent to explain the gospel and baptize.

Nicodemus

John 3:1-21 — A respected religious leader who came to Jesus by night with honest questions and heard the call to be born again.

Cornelius

Acts 10:1-48 — A devout Gentile whom God had already prepared, and to whom Peter was sent so his whole household could hear and believe.

Conclusion

Have you identified a Nicodemus, a Cornelius, the Eunuch, or a Lydia in your own life?

Will you be an Andrew and a Philip — the one who takes hold of Peter and Nathanael and brings them to Jesus?
