Common Obstacles to Sharing the Gospel

Before we can lead others, we need to honestly identify what holds us back. These are the most common obstacles:

  • Fear of rejection
  • Lack of understanding
  • A lack of know-how
  • Procrastination
  • Culture

What other obstacles do you know? Take a moment to name them — naming them is the first step to overcoming them.

The Indisputable Statement

The only way for any soul to become a follower of Jesus is through believing in the gospel.

Acts 4:12

Acts 4:12 (NKJV)

"Nor is there salvation in any other, for there is no other name under heaven given among men by which we must be saved."

What Is the Gospel?

Paul defines it clearly in 1 Corinthians 15:1–4:

1 Corinthians 15:1–4 (NKJV)

1 Moreover, brethren, I declare to you the gospel which I preached to you, which also you received and in which you stand,

2 by which also you are saved, if you hold fast that word which I preached to you — unless you believed in vain.

3 For I delivered to you first of all that which I also received: that Christ died for our sins according to the Scriptures,

4 and that He was buried, and that He rose again the third day according to the Scriptures.

Leading people to Jesus was never meant to be complicated. It is the power of a clear message delivered with faith.

Romans 1:16 (NKJV)

"For I am not ashamed of the gospel of Christ, for it is the power of God to salvation for everyone who believes, for the Jew first and also for the Greek."

Key Pointers for the Personal Evangelist

  • First believe that it is not difficult.
  • Believe that the Father has prepared meetings with non-believers who are ready to follow Jesus — and you will find them.
  • Have faith in God that because Jesus said to "Go," He will be with you.
  • We are to do the "work of an evangelist." 2 Timothy 4:5
2 Timothy 4:5 (NKJV)

"But you be watchful in all things, endure afflictions, do the work of an evangelist, fulfill your ministry."

Key Non-Negotiables

When leading a soul to Jesus, these four elements must be present:

  • The candidate must hear the message of the Kingdom of God. Romans 10:14
  • They should confess with their mouth the Lord Jesus and believe in their hearts that God raised Him from the dead. Romans 10:9–13
  • Repent of their sins, be baptized in water by immersion, and receive the Holy Spirit. Acts 2:38
  • Become a committed member of the local assembly of believers and a follower of the doctrine of Christ. Acts 2:42
Romans 10:9–13 (NKJV)

9 that if you confess with your mouth the Lord Jesus and believe in your heart that God has raised Him from the dead, you will be saved.

10 For with the heart one believes unto righteousness, and with the mouth confession is made unto salvation.

11 For the Scripture says, "Whoever believes on Him will not be put to shame."

12 For there is no distinction between Jew and Greek, for the same Lord over all is rich to all who call upon Him.

13 For "whoever calls on the name of the Lord shall be saved."

Acts 2:38 (NKJV)

"Then Peter said to them, 'Repent, and let every one of you be baptized in the name of Jesus Christ for the remission of sins; and you shall receive the gift of the Holy Spirit.'"

Three Distinct Things in Theology and Practice

Orthodoxy

Right belief — what the Scripture commands.

In Acts 2:38, Peter tells the non-believers exactly what they need to do. Three clear items emerge: Repent, Be Baptized, and Receive the Holy Spirit.

Orthopraxis

Right practice — what the early church actually did.

Acts 8:14–16 shows us these things in action — that the Word was received, and the Spirit was given through the laying on of hands. Orthodoxy and orthopraxis together form the full picture of what it means to bring someone into the Kingdom.

Acts 8:14–16 (NKJV)

14 Now when the apostles who were at Jerusalem heard that Samaria had received the word of God, they sent Peter and John to them,

15 who, when they had come down, prayed for them that they might receive the Holy Spirit.

16 For as yet He had fallen upon none of them. They had only been baptized in the name of the Lord Jesus.

Leading a Soul to Jesus

Follow these steps when you have an opportunity to lead someone to Christ:

  1. Ask the Lord for boldness.
  2. Pray in faith for God to open the candidate's heart.
  3. Look for an open door in conversation.
  4. Offer what you have in Jesus — share your own testimony of how He changed your life.
  5. Share the gospel clearly and simply.
  6. Ask if they are interested in taking God's offer of salvation to become a child of God.
  7. Lead the candidate in a prayer of faith.

Sample Prayer of Faith in Jesus Christ

When the person is ready, lead them through a prayer like this, phrase by phrase:

Prayer of Faith

"Heavenly Father, I come to You in the name of Jesus. I admit that I am a sinner and need Your forgiveness. I believe that Jesus died for my sins, and you raised Him up from among the dead. I ask Jesus to come into my heart and be my Lord and Savior. Thank You for saving me. Amen."
