On Sunday we focused on the gifts of the Holy Spirit and baptism in Him. In this reflection we unpack the baptism of the Holy Spirit in our lives and seek to discern what gifts we have and what gifts we will ask the Lord for.

Baptism of the Holy Spirit

At Pentecost Peter called the crowd, and us, to repent, be baptised (believe) in Jesus for forgiveness and the Holy Spirit will be given and received (Acts 2: 38); this is true for all believers (Acts 2: 39). This is the fulfilment of Jesus’, John’s and Paul’s words explaining that they and we would be baptised by the Spirit into the body of Christ (Matt. 3: 11, Acts 1: 5 & 1 Cor. 12: 13).

Once the Holy Spirit lives within us there can be times when we experience a further infilling of the Holy Spirit for a specific need or event (Luke 1: 67, Acts 4: 8). The infilling of the Holy Spirit can also help us to grow in Christlikeness (Acts 6: 3, 11: 24 & Gal. 5: 22-23).

The Gifts of the Holy Spirit

When we consider the gifts God bestows, we see that they are given to all, for the common good, blessing of the Church and God’s glory; they are to be used not lost (1 Cor. 12: 4-7, 11 & Matt 25: 14-30). There are gifts of service, working (activities and/or abilities) and spiritual gifts. We saw that there are nine gifts of the Spirit, three each within the three categories of Revelation, Vocal and Power gifts (1 Cor. 12: 8-10).

These gifts are now all detailed below. Consider each one, can you think of examples in the New Testament? Ask the Lord to show you if they are a gift you have or a gift He would like you to have? What is the gift to be used for?

Stepping Out

The Lord wants us to desire these gifts, especially the greater ones (1 Cor. 12: 31). We are to ask for them persistently and in faith (Luke 11: 1-13). Once received we are called to use them by remaining in Him and producing fruit for His glory (Matt 25: 14-30, John 15: 7-8).

“Therefore you do not lack any spiritual gift as you eagerly wait for our Lord Jesus Christ to be revealed.” 1 Corinthians 1: 7
